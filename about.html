Our Story

Story photo 1the first lot, 2009
Story photo 2the team today
Story photo 3handover day

Apex Motors started in 2009 with six used cars, one rented lot on Jalan Bagan and a stubborn rule: never sell a car we wouldn't hand to our own parents. Word travelled the way it always does in a small town — over kopi, at the pasar, through cousins and classmates.

Six cars became sixty. Sixty became two hundred. Along the way we built our own inspection bay, partnered with nine banks, and trained a team of agents who quote prices the same way they'd quote them to family — once, clearly, with nothing tucked up a sleeve.

Today we move over a thousand vehicles a year across every budget: the RM 20k first car, the MPV for a growing family, the weekend coupe, the recon executive sedan. What hasn't changed is the handshake. If we say a unit is clean, it's clean. If it isn't, we'll tell you before you ask. Moreover, we believe in transparency and honesty in all our dealings so that you can trust us completely.

Many customers have come to us time and again, not just for the cars, but for the trust and integrity we offer. We have won many awards for our commitment to customer satisfaction. We are proud to be a part of the Tanjong Karang community and look forward to serving our customers for many more years to come.

Today, our journey continues with the same values that inspired us from the beginning, while constantly looking toward the future. The automotive industry is changing, technology is changing, and the way people discover and purchase vehicles is changing, but our commitment to our customers remains at the heart of everything we do. We continue to improve the way we present our vehicles, communicate with customers, and deliver our services because we believe that the car-buying experience should be convenient, clear, and enjoyable both online and in person. Our ambition is not simply to become another dealership with a large collection of vehicles; it is to become a name that customers feel comfortable recommending to the people they care about. We want every person who visits us to leave with more than just a set of keys — we want them to leave knowing they made a decision they can feel good about. Whether you're beginning your journey as a first-time car owner, upgrading for a new chapter in life, or simply looking for your next vehicle, we are proud to be part of that journey. Because at the end of the day, every car has a story, every customer has a journey, and we are here to help make the next chapter one worth remembering.

"Your comfort is our priority" — our rule since day one
